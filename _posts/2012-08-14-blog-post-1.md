Over this past summer, I have been learning about the theory of hyperbolic partial differential equations. The following document are the notes I wrote down while doing so. These notes are strictly based on the lecture notes by professors Jonathan Luk and Gustav Holzegel, and on various sections from Evans' *Partial Differential Equations* (2nd Ed.). Any errors in these documents are entirely my responsibility. Where possible, I have tried to make various computations and justifications as explicit as possible. In the fall, I plan on adding a section on applications of hyperbolic PDE theory to gas dynamics.
